# Baseline specification lab: results

Generated by `src/models/m08_report.py` from `outputs/models/tables/`. Scripts `src/models/m01 ... m07`. These are diagnostic regressions used to choose a specification, not final results.

**Set-up.** Outcome: log median nominal price per m2. Treatment x: new-builds per 1,000 baseline households completed in the previous year (`newbuilds_lag1_per_1000`). Moderators are England-wide z-scores. Sample: main sample 2016-2023, LADs with at least 2 MSOAs (54,312 MSOA-years, 6,789 MSOAs, 294 LADs). Standard errors clustered by LAD unless stated. M3 = MSOA + year FE; M6 = MSOA + LAD x year FE.

**How to read a coefficient.** A coefficient of 0.00005 means that one additional new-build per 1,000 households (previous year) is associated with a 0.0050% difference in the median nominal price per m2 (log-point coefficient x 100, approximately), holding the fixed effects in the model constant. One standard deviation of x is about 0.087% on the same scale (`pct_change_per_1sd_x`: the effect of a one-SD increase in x, 17.6 new-builds per 1,000 households). For an interaction, the coefficient on x*z is the change in the per-unit coefficient for an MSOA one England-wide standard deviation higher on that moderator; multiplied by 100 it gives the change in the percentage effect per additional new-build per 1,000 households, in percentage points.

## 9. Notes on this build

- The outcome is built from nominal prices; year effects absorb the common nominal trend.
- Completions in 2023 are incomplete (UPRN geography matching and EPC lodgement lag), which affects leads that reach 2023.
- Moderators are fixed at baseline; their main effects are absorbed by MSOA fixed effects.
- Place-based funding variables are not included.
