Generated by `src/eda/eda_07_report.py` from the tables in `outputs/eda/tables/`. Scripts: `src/eda/eda_01 ... eda_06`.
The report states what each analysis found; it does not draw policy conclusions. Units are given where they matter.

**Reading notes.** The outcome is `log_median_ppsqm`, the log of the median **nominal** price per m2 in an MSOA-year (not CPIH-deflated). The treatment is new-build completions per 1,000 baseline (2011) households. Main sample = 2016-2023; the full panel is 2012-2023. Quartile 4 of deprivation = most deprived; income quartile 1 = poorest.
